Research

Robots are trained in made-up worlds and then fail in real ones. I build digital twins from real scans, and test robots on real hardware, so the gap shows up before deployment. Every paper has a short explainer video: press play on its figure.

Next: full papers. These are workshop papers. Over the coming year I am extending each one into a full paper for a main venue: ICRA, RSS, IEEE RA-L and T-RO.

  1. A real copper mine, rebuilt for robots to learn in.

    Full title and authors

    MineXplore: An Open-Source Reinforcement Learning Exploration Benchmark for GNSS-Denied Underground Environment

    Abhishek S*, Badrikanath Praharaj*, Sreeram M.V.* * equal contribution

    Mining RL had no benchmark. We built an open-source MuJoCo simulation of a real Chilean copper mine with a contour-to-MJCF pipeline.

  2. 351 metres of real metro tunnel, and no GPS.

    Full title and authors

    Subterra: An Open-Source Validated Benchmark for GNSS-Denied Tunnel Navigation

    Badrikanath Praharaj, Abhishek S, Sreeram M.V.

    Tunnel navigation had no RL testbed with real geometry, so its difficulty was invented. We rebuilt one from LiDAR scans and checked it against 199 metrics.

  3. A cheap robot that notices when its LiDAR is wrong.

    Full title and authors

    Teaching Robots to Say “I Don’t Know”: SENTINEL for Uncertainty-Aware SLAM

    Abhishek S*, Badrikanath Praharaj*, Sreeram M.V.* * equal contribution

    Cheap LiDARs are fooled by glass and mirrors without noticing, and SLAM quietly corrupts the map. SENTINEL checks each scan against depth from a second sensor.

    • <$300robots
    • 5failure modes
    • 0training needed
    • ICRA 2026 · UOWR workshop
    • arXiv
    • PDF

  4. How long will the robots really take?

    Full title and authors

    CADENCE: Predicting Realized MAPF Execution Time Beyond Sum of Costs

    Abhishek S*, Badrikanath Praharaj*, Sreeram M.V.*, Mohan Prakash V * equal contribution

    Multi-robot plans are scored by sum of costs, which is informative but incomplete about how long they take on real robots. Adding a plan's motion burden cuts the prediction error sharply.

    • 480hardware trials
    • 7robots
    • 48.6–59.8%lower held-out MAE
    • ICRA 2026 · MARS workshop
    • arXiv
    • PDF

About

I've been building robots since my second year of college, with one goal: automate the physical world, starting with its hardest places.

What I enjoy most is owning the whole stack: the simulator, the controller, the policy, and the hardware that has to survive contact with reality. My bias is simple. I'd rather ship a benchmark someone can break than a number nobody can reproduce.

Away from the lab, I go looking for rough terrain myself: hiking, trekking up mountains, and riding Karnataka’s back roads with a camera in the bag. It’s usually the kind of ground I’d like robots to handle one day.
